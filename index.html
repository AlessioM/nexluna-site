You never fly your ship.
You program it.

Snap together code blocks, launch into a gravity well full of rival ships and watch your program fight. Every burn bends your orbit. Every shot curves around the planet.

Play it right here

The full game runs in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone held sideways. The first load is about 10 MB. Your progress is saved in this browser only, separate from the Android app.

  • Drag blocks into your program
  • Click a value to edit it
  • Launch and watch it fight

Pasting a challenge code? The browser may ask once for clipboard access.

From blocks to battle

  1. 1

    Build

    Drag motion, sensing, operator, control and combat blocks into a program. No typing, no syntax errors — just ideas.

  2. 2

    Launch

    Your ship enters orbit and runs your code on its own. You can't steer it anymore. Gravity, fuel and ammo are all that matter now.

  3. 3

    Watch & refine

    Active blocks light up while the battle runs. Pause, speed up or scrub the replay to see exactly why your plan worked — or flew into a planet.

Everything in orbit

50-level campaign

Ten chapters teach one idea at a time — from staying alive to bent bullets, moons, squads and champions. Earn up to three stars per level.

Watch your program think

The running block glows during the match. Pause, play at 2×, fit the arena on screen or scrub back through a replay.

Real orbital physics

Every burn changes your orbit and shells fall under gravity too. Moons, crowded arenas, scarce fuel and a shrinking sudden-death ring keep it fresh.

Challenge your friends

Build an arena ship and send its challenge code through any messenger. Matches are deterministic: same ships, same result on every device.

AI tournaments

Throw your ship into round-robin tournaments against imported rivals and built-in champions, then replay any match from the table.

No account, plays offline

Just install and play — no sign-up, no login, no connection needed. Your programs and progress are saved on your phone.
